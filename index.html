ABOUT THE FIRM

Professional insight.
Practical direction.

Corporate & Legal Professionals LLP is a professionally managed firm established in 2017. We support businesses, entrepreneurs and corporate clients across corporate secretarial services, regulatory compliance, company law and legal advisory.

Our work spans ROC/MCA matters, listed-entity and SEBI compliance, contract drafting and review, due diligence, title searches and allied legal services. We aim to make complex requirements easier to understand and manage through diligent, commercially aware support.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions lead
to clearer next steps.

Here are some common starting points. The answer for your matter may depend on the entity, facts and current requirements.

Ask our team
What information should I share for a company compliance query?

Share the entity type, relevant dates, a brief description of the requirement and any notices or filing references. Avoid sending confidential documents until a secure sharing method and engagement are agreed.

Can you help with company incorporation?

We can discuss your proposed business structure, documentation and incorporation-related filing support. The appropriate route and requirements depend on your circumstances and applicable rules.

Do you assist with listed-company and SEBI compliance?

Our listed-entity compliance practice includes support relating to applicable SEBI regulations, disclosures, governance reporting and compliance processes. The scope is confirmed after reviewing the entity and requirement.

Can you review a contract before signing?

We can discuss review of commercial agreements, key obligations, risk allocation and provisions such as payment, termination, confidentiality and dispute resolution.

How do I get started?

Contact us by email or phone with a short summary of your requirement. We can then discuss relevant documents, scope, availability, timelines and engagement terms.

Do you guarantee a filing outcome or legal result?

No. Outcomes depend on the facts, applicable law, authorities and other circumstances. Any proposed scope and expected process should be discussed before engagement.
