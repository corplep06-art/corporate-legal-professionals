Corporate & Legal Professionals LLP is a professionally managed firm incorporated in 2017.

We specialize in corporate secretarial services, regulatory compliance, corporate law and legal advisory. We provide comprehensive and practical solutions to businesses, entrepreneurs and corporate clients, helping them navigate statutory and regulatory requirements with confidence.

We value integrity, diligence and enduring professional relationships, with solutions tailored to each client's needs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Some common questions

Need help with a specific situation? Contact our team to discuss the details.

What information should I share for a first enquiry?

Share your organisation type, the service you need, any relevant deadline and a brief description of the issue. Avoid sending confidential documents until a secure sharing method has been agreed.

Can you help with ROC/MCA filings and annual compliance?

The firm offers corporate secretarial and ROC/MCA compliance support. The exact scope depends on your entity type, filing status and requirements.

Can I ask about SEBI or listed-company compliance?

Yes. You can contact the team with a brief description of the entity and the regulatory matter. The applicable obligations and timelines should be confirmed for your specific circumstances.

How do I request contract review or legal advisory?

Use the enquiry form and select the relevant area. Mention the type of agreement or issue and any important timeline. Do not include highly sensitive information in the initial message.

Does submitting this website form automatically book a consultation?

No. This static website form prepares an email in your email application; it does not itself book appointments or store submissions. A consultation is arranged after the firm responds.
